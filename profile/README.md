# Feathery — AI Operating & Decisioning Platform for Financial Services

Feathery is the AI operating and decisioning system purpose-built for financial services. Insurance and wealth management firms use Feathery to automate data collection, document processing, onboarding, and operational workflows — without replacing the systems they already run.

**Website:** [https://www.feathery.io](https://www.feathery.io)

## Overview

Financial services teams don't have one workflow problem. They have hundreds — across client segments, products, and lines of business. Generic AI tools struggle with regulated complexity; point solutions only fix one narrow task.

Feathery sits in between: a platform flexible enough to power the client and policy lifecycle, and specialized enough for insurance and wealth management compliance, data formats, and integrations.

- **10M+** workflows processed
- **150+** industry-specific integrations
- **300+** firms across insurance and wealth management
- Founded in **2021**, based in San Francisco

---

## What we build

### AI Operating System
Collects and structures client information, syncs systems of record, and normalizes data across surfaces — forms, documents, and operational flows.

### AI Decisioning System
Analyzes business data flowing through the platform, surfaces insights and recommendations, and feeds those learnings back into workflows to drive more automation.

### Robin
Robin is Feathery's AI workflow assistant. Teams describe what they need in plain language; Robin builds and deploys the workflow — forms, logic, integrations, and document flows. Once live, Robin keeps working: answer questions, surface book-of-business insights, recommend next-best actions, and flag exceptions. No engineering required.

## How it works

1. **Describe your workflow** — Tell Robin what the team needs in plain language.
2. **Robin builds it** — Forms, logic, integrations, and doc flows are generated for review and approval.
3. **Robin runs with your team** — Ask questions, get recommendations, and keep workflows improving after go-live.

Most customers get a first workflow live in minutes, with hands-on support from Feathery's implementation team.
